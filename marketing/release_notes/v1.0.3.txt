WCS Care 1.0.3 — Plans, Pricing & Pilot Validation

This build turns on the monetization validation layer for TestFlight.

WHAT'S NEW
• Plans & Pricing screen with three clear tiers:
  – Starter (free) for family carers
  – Care Pro (~$9.99/mo) for individual & professional carers
  – Care Team (custom) for clinics & care organizations
• Care Pro waitlist with email capture
• Care Team pilot request form for organizations
• Beta analytics: plan-tap demand signals, feedback events, growth roadmap view
• 12-Month Growth Roadmap screen reachable from Plans & Pricing
• In-app links to TestFlight invite, pilot form, support email and the WCS website

HOW YOU CAN HELP
• Tap any plan that interests you — we use those taps to decide what to build first.
• If your organization wants a pilot, open Settings → Plans & Pricing → Request Pilot.
• Share Beta Feedback any time from Plans & Pricing.

KNOWN
• All paid features are free during TestFlight. We're learning, not selling.
